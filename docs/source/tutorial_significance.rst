Compare Recovery and Background
===============================

Use the prepared sky-mask catalogs to compare recovery, or reuse the
public-data interval to calculate a small time-slide background. For matching
recovered and missed sources, first complete :doc:`tutorial_population`.

.. toctree::
   :maxdepth: 1

For training and efficiency on your own completed catalogs, follow
:doc:`postproduction_study` under :doc:`run_analyses`. The small tutorial samples
demonstrate data flow; they do not supply a training or sensitivity study.
Statistical explanations are in :doc:`core_concepts`, and action signatures
are in :doc:`postproduction_actions`.
